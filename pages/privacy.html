Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This policy explains what information youtubethumbgrabber.xyz ("we," "us," "the site") collects when you use the thumbnail downloader tool, and how that information is handled. By using the site, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We don't require an account, and we don't ask you to enter any personal information to use the tool. Specifically:

  • The YouTube link you paste is processed in your browser to extract a video ID — it is not logged or stored on our servers.
  • We do not store copies of the thumbnail images you view or download. They're fetched directly from YouTube's public image servers (img.youtube.com) each time.
  • Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical data — IP address, browser type, pages visited, timestamps — for security and diagnostic purposes.

Cookies and third-party services

This site uses a small number of third-party services that may set their own cookies or collect data according to their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdSense — we display ads through Google AdSense, which may use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can review or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings.
  • Google Fonts — fonts are loaded from Google's font servers, which may log requests including IP address.
  • jsDelivr CDN — used to load our styling library, which may log basic request data.
  • YouTube / img.youtube.com — thumbnail images are requested directly from YouTube's servers, subject to Google's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings, and use your browser's privacy or ad-blocking tools to limit tracking by third-party services listed above.

Changes to this policy
