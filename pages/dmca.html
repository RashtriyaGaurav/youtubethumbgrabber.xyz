Our position on hosted content

youtubethumbgrabber.xyz does not host, upload, cache, or store thumbnail images, videos, or any other copyrighted media. The Site simply links to and displays images that are already publicly served by YouTube's own image servers (img.youtube.com) for any given public video. If you believe a thumbnail should be removed from YouTube itself, that request needs to go to YouTube directly, since we don't control that content.
