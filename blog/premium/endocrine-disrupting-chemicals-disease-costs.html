Exposure to Chemicals Dangerous to Hormone Function Burdens Americans With Hundreds of Billions in Disease Costs

TLDR

  • Financial Impact: Routine exposure to everyday endocrine disrupting chemicals costs the US over $340 billion annually in healthcare expenses and lost productivity (over 2.3% of US GDP).
  • Primary Culprits: Flame retardants (PBDEs), pesticides, bisphenol A (BPA), and phthalates found in plastic containers, metal cans, cosmetics, and packaging.
  • Neurological Damage: PBDEs and pesticides cause nearly two-thirds of the total damage, primarily through fetal brain impairment leading to 12.8 million lost IQ points and thousands of cases of intellectual disability, autism, and ADHD each year.
  • Adult & Reproductive Health: Phthalate exposures are linked to 86,000 cases of endometriosis ($47B+ cost), thousands of cases of obesity and diabetes, and 10,700 premature cardiovascular deaths annually.
  • Risk Reduction: Researchers recommend simple home protocols: avoid microwaving plastic, hand-wash plastic containers, avoid plastics labeled with recycling numbers 3, 6, or 7, and switch to fragrance-free cosmetics.
