YouTube thumbnail sizes, explained

Every YouTube video can have up to five thumbnail sizes. Not every video has all five — older or low-resolution uploads sometimes skip the biggest one — but here's what each size is normally used for.

Name Dimensions Good for
Master (maxresdefault) Up to 1280 × 720 Blog headers, presentations, anything printed large
Standard (sddefault) 640 × 480 Website previews and article thumbnails
High (hqdefault) 480 × 360 The one YouTube itself uses most often
Medium (mqdefault) 320 × 180 Small embeds and lightweight pages
Small (default) 120 × 90 Icons, search result previews

Frequently asked questions

Is this YouTube thumbnail downloader really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no watermark, and no limit on how many thumbnails you download.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in your browser on desktop or mobile — the only thing that gets downloaded is the image itself.

Why is the HD (maxresdefault) version sometimes missing?

Older videos and some uploaded straight from a phone never had a full HD thumbnail generated for them. Every other size will still be there.

Does this work for YouTube Shorts?

Yes — paste the Shorts link the same way you'd paste a regular video link.

Is it legal to download a YouTube thumbnail?

Thumbnails are served from YouTube's own public image servers, so saving one for personal or editorial use is generally fine. The image still belongs to the video owner, so check before using it commercially or republishing it elsewhere.
