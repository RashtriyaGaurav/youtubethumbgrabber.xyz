Why Overlays Make Such a Difference

Overlays are one of the fastest ways to make an edit feel more polished without learning complex compositing. A light leak over a transition, a bit of grain to warm up flat footage, or a glitch effect on a hard cut — small touches like these are what separate a home video from something that looks intentionally directed.

How to Use These Overlays

  • Drag and Drop: Add the overlay clip above your footage on a new track.
  • Set Blend Mode: Screen or Add usually works best for light leaks and particles.
  • Trim to Fit: Line the overlay up with cuts or transitions for the biggest impact.
  • Layer Sparingly: One or two overlays per scene reads as intentional; more than that gets noisy.
